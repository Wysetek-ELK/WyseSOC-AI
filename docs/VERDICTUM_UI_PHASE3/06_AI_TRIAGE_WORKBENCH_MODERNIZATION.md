## 1. Overview & Objectives

The AI Triage Workbench is the frontline investigation surface for SOC analysts reviewing ingested security incidents. Prior to Phase 3, incident detail views suffered from extreme vertical sprawl, mixed verdict signals, and overwhelming technical telemetry dumped above the fold.

Phase 3 introduces:
1. The **4-Dimension Incident Verdict Matrix**, cleanly disambiguating all platform determinations.
2. An **Operational Next Action Card**, telling the analyst exactly what step is required.
3. Collapsible **Advanced Technical Diagnostics**, moving token usage, cryptographic signatures, and missing telemetry tables into an expandable panel.
4. Preserved **Double-Blind Mode**, allowing unbiased incident reviews.

---

## 2. The 4-Dimension Incident Verdict Matrix

Analysts frequently confused the raw output of the LLM with the platform-validated verdict or the analyst ground truth. The `IncidentVerdictMatrix` presents a unified four-part comparative grid:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  INCIDENT VERDICT MATRIX                                          |
+--------------------------+--------------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| 1. AI PROPOSED VERDICT   | 2. PLATFORM VALIDATED    | 3. ANALYST VERIFIED   | 4. SIEM OPERATIONAL |
| TRUE POSITIVE            | REQUIRES ATTENTION       | PENDING REVIEW        | OPEN                |
| (Uncalibrated LLM: 88%)  | (Validation: REJECTED)   | (Double-Blind Masked) | (FortiSIEM: Active) |
+--------------------------+--------------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
```

### Dimensional Breakdown:
1. **AI Proposed Verdict:** The initial, uncalibrated threat classification generated by the local Qwen/vLLM model.
2. **Platform Validated Verdict:** The authoritative platform determination following deterministic evidence contract checks, claim grounding ratios, and hallucination neutralization. If the model cited ungrounded telemetry, this will show `REQUIRES_ATTENTION` with a clear override alert.
3. **Analyst Verified Verdict:** The final human-in-the-loop disposition recorded by a SOC analyst. In Double-Blind mode, this remains masked until review submission.
4. **Operational SIEM Status:** The live resolution status in FortiSIEM (e.g. `Manually Cleared`, `System Closed`, `Unresolved`).

---

## 3. Dynamic Operational Next Action Directive

Positioned directly below the Verdict Matrix, the `NextActionCard` dynamically computes the appropriate operational action:
- **No Verdict Generated Yet:** Displays *"Generate Automated AI Verdict: Run the local LLM Triage Agent against canonical evidence to evaluate threat disposition"* with a 1-click execution button.
- **Verdict Flagged (`REQUIRES_ATTENTION`):** Displays *"Investigate Flagged Inconsistencies: Model proposed verdict was withheld due to ungrounded claims or missing required evidence. Analyst validation required."*
- **Awaiting Ground Truth:** Displays *"Record Analyst Ground Truth: AI classified as [VERDICT] ([CONFIDENCE]%). Confirm or override in Human Review Queue."* with a quick link to the review tab.
- **Ground Truth Recorded:** Displays *"Review Synchronized Ground Truth: Ground truth resolution recorded as '[RESOLUTION]'. FortiSIEM status is '[STATUS]'."*

---

## 4. Progressive Disclosure & Collapsible Diagnostics

Deep technical telemetry and diagnostic logs that previously cluttered the page are now organized into accessible progressive disclosure panels:
- **Primary View (Always Visible):**
  - Authoritative Platform Verdict Card (Verdict, effective confidence, trust score).
  - Analytical Justification & Grounding Reasoning.
  - Analyst Action Directive.
  - Core Network Entities & Investigation Details.
- **Advanced Details Panel (Collapsible on demand):**
  - Validation Assessment Codes (e.g. `PASSED_EVIDENCE_SUFFICIENCY`, `CLAIM_UNGROUNDED`).
  - Unsupported / Fabricated Claims Table (field, asserted value, grounding status).
  - Missing Required Telemetry & Field Mapping Diagnostics (`NOT_MAPPED`, `CONTEXT_OMITTED`).
  - Unmapped Source Event Attributes (raw SIEM attributes).
  - Supporting Evidence Attributes.
  - Verified Historical Precedents (Semantic RAG tenant-isolated matches).

---

## 5. Non-Negotiable Safeguards Preserved

1. **Zero Data Leakage:** Ground truth closure fields (`resolution`, `clearedReason`, `comments`) remain strictly excluded from model input payloads.
2. **Double-Blind Mode:** When active, the Historical Ground Truth tab and SIEM resolution are masked from view.
3. **FortiSIEM Synchronization:** The `Sync SIEM` action allows analysts to pull live resolution status from FortiSIEM on demand without page reload.
